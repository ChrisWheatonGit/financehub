# FinanceHub v1.2 — Household demo and month picker

- No-Spend Tracker has explicit named-month and year dropdowns plus previous/next navigation. Future days are excluded from the quiet-day count.
- Transactions can be marked Me, Partner, or Shared Household, with filtering and CSV owner information. Shared transactions are counted once in combined totals. Older demo records are treated as Me.
- **This is not real multi-user collaboration.** All entries remain in one browser localStorage namespace. There are no independent accounts, permission boundaries, or cross-device synchronization. Those require backend authentication, a household model, invitations, and database access controls.
- Only fictional data should be used.
